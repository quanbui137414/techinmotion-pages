Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

TechInMotion Uploader ("the Tool") is a private, internal application operated by the owner of the Tech in Motion channel ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Tool handles.

1. What the Tool accesses

With our authorisation, the Tool connects to our own TikTok account through the TikTok API to upload videos and read basic profile information needed to publish them (such as the account's display name and the privacy options available to it).

2. Data we collect

The Tool collects no personal data from anyone. It has no users other than us. It does not track visitors to these pages, set cookies, or use analytics.

3. Data we store

The only data stored is the API access token for our own account, kept on our own computer, and a local record of which videos have been published. No data is sent to any third party other than TikTok itself.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

5. Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Revoking access invalidates the stored token. Locally stored tokens can be deleted by us at any time.

6. Changes

We may update this policy at any time by publishing a new version on this page.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the Tech in Motion channel through TikTok or YouTube (@TechInMotion).
