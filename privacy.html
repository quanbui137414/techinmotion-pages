Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

TechInMotion Uploader ("the Tool") is a private, internal application operated by the owner of the Tech in Motion channel ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the Tool handles.

1. What the Tool accesses

With our authorisation, the Tool connects to our own accounts on YouTube (through YouTube API Services), Facebook and Instagram (through the Meta Graph API) and TikTok (through the TikTok API) to upload videos, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, and to read the basic profile information needed to publish them (such as the account's name and the privacy options available to it). It never reads other people's data.

2. Data we collect

The Tool collects no personal data from anyone. It has no users other than us. It does not track visitors to these pages, set cookies, or use analytics.

3. Data we store

The only data stored is the API access tokens for our own accounts, kept on our own computer, and a local record of which videos have been published (their IDs and links). No data is sent to any third party other than the platform the video is being published to. Data obtained from YouTube API Services is not stored beyond the IDs of our own uploads, and no data is shared with anyone. Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

5. Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time: for Google/YouTube at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for Facebook and Instagram in Meta Accounts Center under Business integrations, and for TikTok in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Revoking access invalidates the stored token. Locally stored tokens can be deleted by us at any time.

6. Changes

We may update this policy at any time by publishing a new version on this page.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the Tech in Motion channel through TikTok or YouTube (@TechInMotion).
